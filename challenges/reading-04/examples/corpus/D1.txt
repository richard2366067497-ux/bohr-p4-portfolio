Methylene blue removal on TiO2 P25 (runnable example material)

Methylene blue (MB) at 10 mg/L was degraded over TiO2 P25 (0.5 g/L) under 365 nm UV light.
After 120 min at pH 3 the removal efficiency reached 95%, with an equilibrium concentration
Ce of 0.5 mg/L.

At pH 9 the removal efficiency fell to 62% (Ce = 3.8 mg/L), consistent with weaker adsorption
of the cationic dye at alkaline pH.
