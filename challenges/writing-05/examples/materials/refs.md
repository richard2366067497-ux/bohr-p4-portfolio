# Source material (citable snippets)

[1] Smith et al. A randomised controlled trial of caffeine on vigilance (n = 240). Journal of
Attention Research, 2019, 12(3): 210-219.

[2] A prospective cohort study of 1,200 nurses reported that caffeine was associated with higher
sustained attention after a night shift.

[3] Lee et al. A cross-sectional survey (n = 480) found no association between caffeine dose and
reaction time in well-rested students.

[4] A meta-analysis of 21 randomised trials reported a small but significant improvement in
reaction time.
