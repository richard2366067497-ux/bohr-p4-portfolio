# Draft material: composite coating pilot study (example)

## Method

Forty-eight adults were randomised 1:1 to an experimental composite coating or a control coating.

## Results

Mean surface roughness fell by 18.0% in the experimental group (95% CI 10.2%–25.8%; P=0.004) at 12 weeks.

The control group showed no statistically significant change (P=0.37).

## Discussion

Bacterial adhesion was lower with the experimental coating (2.4 x 10^5 vs 3.1 x 10^5 CFU/cm2).

The study was not powered to assess clinical infection.
