# Effect of cooling on weld quality

We ask whether cooling rate is associated with weld quality in thermoplastic laminates.

## Introduction

Cooling rate has been associated with weld quality in prior observational studies of
thermoplastic joints, and the fill compositing technique is reported to improve joint
strength in the same family of materials.

## Method

Specimens were cooled along a fixed ramp and the weld quality was measured for every
run along that ramp. Cooling rate is associated with weld quality across the tested
range in this observational study of thermoplastic joints.

## Results

The cooling rate is associated with weld quality across all tested specimens in this observational study of thermoplastic joints, and the measured difference stayed stable across every replicate run.
