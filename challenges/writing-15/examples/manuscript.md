# Example manuscript (illustrative, two chapters)

This example is planted with one drift of each kind so the ledgers and findings are non-empty.

## 1. Methods

Fused deposition modelling (FDM) was used to print every specimen. The build angle is defined as
the angle between the part axis and the build plate. The nozzle moved at 45 mm/min and the chamber
stayed at 60 °C. The layer height was set to 0.2 mm, where L is the layer height in the slicing
profile. Strength reached 45.2 MPa in the first run.

The build angle is defined as the angle between the deposited bead and the horizontal plane. A
second batch used FDM (fused deposition manufacturing) with the same parameters. Figure 3 shows the
measured force trace, and the calibration follows the procedure in [5]. The scan speed is written
as 45 mm min-1 in the log. Here L is the specimen length in the fixture.

## 2. Results and discussion

The angled specimens show a significant increase in strength. Table 1 lists the mean and the
standard deviation, and Figure 1 gives the force trace. The measured strength of 45.2MPa is
consistent with the first run.

The angled specimens show no significant increase in strength once the fixture offset was
removed. The strength result therefore supports a conditional reading rather than a general claim.
