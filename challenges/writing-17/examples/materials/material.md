# Example material: hydrothermal TiO2 (fictional mind-map input)

## Experimental design

Three temperature groups (120, 160, 200 °C) with three independent batches each; reaction time 12 h.

## Results

Mean particle size was 18 nm at 120 °C, 25 nm at 160 °C and 41 nm at 200 °C.

Mean yield was 72% at 120 °C, 86% at 160 °C and 84% at 200 °C.

## Notes

The 160 °C batch yields were 84%, 86% and 88%.

One record states a 200 °C mean yield of 91%; the original weighing sheet is not attached.
