# Example material: furfural selective hydrogenation (fictional)

## 1 Introduction

Cat-A gave 62.0% conversion and 88.0% furfuryl-alcohol selectivity at 220 °C.

## 2 Method

Cat-B was evaluated in three independent preparation-evaluation batches at 220 °C.

## 3 Results

Batch conversions were 64.0%, 65.0% and 66.0%; selectivities were 88.5%, 89.0% and 89.5%.

## 4 Discussion
