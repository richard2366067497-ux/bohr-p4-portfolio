# Example material: ZrO2-coated NCM811 (fictional)

## Background

Historical uncoated NCM811 cells retained 82.0%, 83.0% and 84.0% of capacity after 100 cycles.

## Objective

Test whether a 2 wt% ZrO2 coating reaches a mean retention of at least 90.0% under the same conditions.

## Plan

Cells are cycled at 25 °C, 0.5C, 2.8-4.3 V; three independent batches are planned.

## Budget

Materials 400 CNY per batch, XRD 80 CNY per batch, cell testing 250 CNY per batch.
