Effect of Rice Genotype qDTY12.1 on Grain Yield under Drought (example material)

Abstract

Drought is a major constraint on rainfed rice production. This example material is a
short synthetic note used only to demonstrate the reading-client closed loop.

Introduction

Conventional breeding has produced lines that carry the drought-yield quantitative
trait locus qDTY12.1. Yield advantages are usually reported as percentages relative
to a susceptible recurrent parent.

Methods

Trials were conducted in two seasons with three replications per genotype. Grain
yield was recorded per plot and converted to t/ha at 14% moisture.

Results

Rice lines carrying qDTY12.1 showed 28% higher grain yield under drought, reaching
4.6 t/ha against 3.6 t/ha for the recurrent parent.

Under irrigated control conditions the yield advantage narrowed to 4%, indicating the
benefit is drought specific.

Conclusion

The locus qDTY12.1 is associated with a consistent yield advantage in rainfed
lowland environments, but the effect size depends on the water regime.
